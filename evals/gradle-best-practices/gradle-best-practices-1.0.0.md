## Findings

**Model tiers used below.** This grouping is an assumption, not something the harness records: *frontier* is `claude-opus-5-5`, `claude-sonnet-5` and `deepseek-v4-pro`. *Entry-level / low-cost* is `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, `gemini-3-8-flash` and `gpt-5-6-luna`; Luna is placed here on its recorded cost. *Local* is `qwen3-6-35b-a3b-coding-nvfp4` on Ollama. Each comparison is one run per side, and identical runs differed on 0–4 of 12 checks in the A/A control (`gemini-3-8-flash` 4, `claude-sonnet-5` and `deepseek-v4-pro` 3), so a difference of a few checks is within noise for those models.

### Best results

- **`deepseek-v4-pro` is the strongest result overall:** 29 → 51 of 51 with the skill, 22 gains, no losses, for $0.872 over all five scenarios.
- **`claude-sonnet-5` is the nearest frontier alternative:** 30 → 50 of 51, but at $6.31, about seven times DeepSeek's cost.
- **`claude-opus-5-5` was already near the ceiling without the skill (45 of 51)** and moved to 48. One of its misses is a scorer limitation: it registered tasks in Java, which the `tasks-documented` scorer that ran did not read (see Notes). For Opus the skill mostly adds cost (2.08×) rather than results.
- **Best value: `gpt-5-6-luna`,** at 27 → 47 of 51 for $0.159 in total, with no run-to-run variation in the control.

### Frontier vs entry-level

This skill does not equalise the way the wrapper skill does. It encodes a broad body of knowledge, not one procedure, and how much of it a model applies depends on the model.

- **Without the skill, scores already spread widely,** from 11 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`) to 45 (`claude-opus-5-5`).
- **With it, the hosted mid and frontier models converge high (46–51).**
- **`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` gains a lot (+20) but stays well behind at 29 of 51.**
- **`gemini-3-8-flash` is the outlier among the cheaper models.** It scored high without the skill (38) and is the only model whose cost and time went *down* with the skill (0.90×, 0.89×). It is also the noisiest model in the control, so its 9-gain difference is less certain than it looks.
- **Cost:** for every other model, the skill made runs longer (wall clock 1.19–1.79×) and more expensive (cost 1.37–2.08×). Models act on more of the advice and do more work.

**Reading for a user:** on a frontier model the skill buys a few more checks for roughly double the cost. On a capable mid-priced model (`deepseek-v4-pro`, `gpt-5-6-luna`), it is the difference between an average result and a near-complete one. On `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` it helps, but it does not make up for the model.

### Local model

`qwen3-6-35b-a3b-coding-nvfp4` went from 11 to 21 of 51, but its 17 gains came with 7 losses, by far the most of any model.

- **Broken builds:** in `idioms` and `plugin-authoring` it left the build broken with the skill (`project-builds` lost in both). In `idioms` it also undid four idioms it had right without the skill.
- **Token limit:** its `plugin-authoring` run with the skill hit the 10M-token limit.
- **Time:** its runs took 2.8 hours in total without the skill and at least 3.6 hours with it (10,167 s and ≥ 12,902 s).

The skill's guidance seems to exceed what this model can apply reliably across a multi-project build. It attempts more changes and breaks things in the process.

**Reading for a user:** with a local model of this class, use the skill for audits (it gained on `false-positives`) or targeted fixes, check that the build still passes, and don't expect frontier-level results.
